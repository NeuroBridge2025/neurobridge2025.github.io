NeuroAdapt

Adaptive AI platform designed to support neurodivergent adults by reducing cognitive overload.

The Problem

Autistic and neurodivergent adults often face sensory overload and limited access to adaptive environments. Existing tools are reactive and rarely designed with neurodivergent-first principles.

The Solution

NeuroAdapt builds adaptive systems that personalise environments in real time using AI, learning individual sensory and behavioural patterns.

Personalised Adaptation

AI models adjust prompts, routines, and sensory inputs based on individual needs.

Neurodivergent-First

Built with direct input from lived experience, not retrofitted later.

MVP

Functional Prototype

Early software-based adaptive assistant developed during the AI’preneur Accelerator.
